# Tomorrow_syndrome
Exploratory Data Analysis of student procrastination patterns, examining phone usage, study habits, exam preparation, and behavioural factors using Python.
The complete Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA), including data cleaning, preprocessing, descriptive statistics, data visualization, correlation analysis, and outlier detection, is available in Google Colab.

## Explore Our Website
We built an interactive website to present our project and make our findings easier to explore.
🔗 [Visit Tomorrow Syndrome](https://glittering-marigold-46d69c.netlify.app/)

## Dataset Collection
Instead of relying only on existing datasets, we decided to collect our own data directly from students.
As part of this project, we created a Google Form and shared it with students at **Vellore Institute of Technology (VIT)** to understand their study habits, phone usage, procrastination patterns, exam preparation, and academic behaviour.
We collected the responses, organized the data, and used it to explore how different factors influence students' academic routines and procrastination.
We have also made our dataset publicly available on Kaggle:
🔗 [Student Procrastination Academic Behavior Dataset – Kaggle](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/jayshrims/student-procrastination-academic-behavior-dataset)

## What This Project Explores
Our project focuses on understanding student procrastination through real survey responses and data analysis.
Some of the key areas we explored include:
- **Data Collection:** Gathering responses from VIT students through a Google Form.
- **Data Cleaning:** Organizing the collected responses and handling missing values.
- **Exploratory Data Analysis:** Identifying patterns in students' academic behaviour.
- **Descriptive Statistics:** Understanding trends through statistical measures.
- **Data Visualization:** Presenting findings through meaningful graphs and charts.
- **Correlation Analysis:** Exploring relationships between phone usage, study habits, and procrastination.
- **Outlier Detection:** Identifying unusual patterns in the collected data.
